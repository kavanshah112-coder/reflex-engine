**K5 — acquisition lifts real-language coverage. FALSIFIED, both

residual gap on real text is multi-step arithmetic COMPOSITION

risk, not coverage. Dimensional gates certify physics, not story

cost ~0.25 ms — the optimization the spec anticipated was
