## 2. The bug chronicle (in full, per protocol)

Both defects surfaced on the FRESH-SEED sealed finals, after first
results; both handled by preserve-supersede-rerun
(`superseded_W1c/`, `sim_superseded_pass2/`):

**B1 — grading adapter refused text-served numeric answers** (19
phantom "wrong answers": correct prose like "3600 seconds" failed a
numeric comparator). Analysis-side re-grade by first-number
extraction; the stored rows were always faithful.

**B2 — a latent ordinal gap, five versions old.** The reference
regex knew ordinals only to "fifth" while the resolution table went
to "tenth". Fresh seed 9666 generated "the kinetic energy of the
tenth one": no reference matched, the solve fell through to GLOBAL,
consumed the FIRST tank's facts, and returned exactly the bind-trap
answer — 1/76 wrong-fire (1.3%) on a class every prior sealed
battery had "passed" because no earlier seed ever asked beyond
fifth. Fixed (ordinals to tenth), PARITY-PLUS green, finals re-run:
sem 100%. The class-level lesson is logged as the arc's top open
safety item: an unresolved reference over a multi-entity state must
abstain, never fall through to a global solve. Seed luck is not
coverage — only vocabulary-complete gates are.
