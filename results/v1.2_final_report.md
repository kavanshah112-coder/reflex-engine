## 1. Executive summary

**Headline (frozen ladder): OUTCOME B — the safety boundary and the
economics transfer to real human language; acquisition does not.
The engine is now formally certified (65-seed sweep, zero
wrong-fire), costs 21 millijoules of marginal compute per certified
answer, and abstains on essentially everything humans wrote that it
was never built for — which is exactly the claim a certified system
must be able to make on first contact with the real world.**

- **W1 Safety certification.** The B2 class is closed: an
  ambiguity-by-default guard (unresolved reference-shaped asks over
  competing entity bindings → hazard abstention) + appositive alias
  resolution that replaced 12 correct-by-luck adversarial answers
  with correct-by-binding ones + a permanent vocabulary-completeness
  audit (2 gaps found: one closed, one documented dead after the
  battery showed the "fix" hijacked working paths) + **seed-sweep
  certification: 10,160 trap items across 65 independent generation
  seeds, wrong-fire 0.000** — including beyond-vocabulary ordinals
  and vague references. `certify.py` regenerates the machine safety
  case (verdict CERTIFIED) and a one-page external certificate.
- **W3 Energy.** The 1.93 J/query scare was the instrument, not the
  engine: clean per-query metering gives **0.356 ± 0.018 J/q**, and
  the batch-window instrument (authoritative at 0.85 ms/query
  compute) gives **0.0211 ± 0.0009 J/q — ~21 mJ of marginal compute
  per certified answer**, ~90,000× under the measured pure-LLM cost.
- **W2 Real language.** First contact with human-written text
  (GSM8K/ASDiv/SciQ, frozen with hashes): zero-shot, the engine
  abstained on 519 of 520 sealed items and wrong-answered ONE
  (0.33%; itemized: correct physics on the wrong reading). The
  acquisition loops then mined 98 (sonnet) and 45+ (opus)
  gate-certified reflexes from real traffic — **and lifted sealed
  coverage by exactly zero in both arms.** The mechanism is measured
  three ways: the gap on real text is multi-step arithmetic
  composition, not vocabulary; the miners' own extraction reports
  said so; and the scale test INVERTED — opus's capability-level
  proposals produced MORE wrong answers than sonnet's lexicon-level
  ones (17 vs 7 in traffic; +1 sealed). **Dimensional gates certify
  physics, not story semantics.** That sentence is V1.3's problem
  statement.
