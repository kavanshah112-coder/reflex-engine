## 1. Executive summary

V1.0 built the single integrated system the arc pointed at: every
surviving layer of V0.1–V0.11 behind one gateway
(`ask() -> CERTIFIED_REFLEX | LLM_ESCALATED | ABSTAINED` with a
certificate on every answer), pluggable into any LLM, with domain
knowledge externalized into a pack and a live acquisition loop
(propose -> gates -> shadow probation -> ACTIVE -> pack overlay).
Then it ran the pre-registered four-arm ablation on a 5,000-query
escalated-residual stream.

**Headline (frozen ladder): OUTCOME C — integration succeeded
completely; the pre-registered acquisition bet (H3, llama-8B miner)
was falsified; and the scale replications show the falsification is
about the miner's model scale, not the architecture.**

- **Integration parity is total.** All five V0.5–V0.11 sealed suites
  reproduce the v0.11 champion item-for-item (zero diffs, three
  times), 196/196 regression at zero LLM calls, invariants 8/8,
  0.403 J/query at 100% (bound 0.61) — and identically through the
  extracted physics pack (13/13 domain structures byte-identical to
  the hardcoded state).
- **The acquisition ablation separated cleanly.** On a stream the
  frozen engine scores exactly 0% on: breeder A1 produced ZERO
  candidates in 5,000 queries (it needs completed parses; residual
  never completes). Local miner A2 filled probation (132 ACTIVE) yet
  lifted nothing — certifiable noise. The same arm re-run with haiku
  mining: **+5.8pp** certified coverage (29/0 vs A0, p≈4e-9); with
  sonnet: **+7.2pp** (36/0, p≈0; sonnet>haiku 7/0, p=0.016).
  Monotone in miner scale.
- **Safety never moved: wrong-fire 0.000 in every gated arm, every
  probe, every sealed final.** Coverage equals accuracy everywhere —
  each arm answers only what it certifies. The fresh adversarial
  final: engine 0.000 vs pure sonnet 25.4%, pure haiku 24.6%, pure
  llama 36.9% wrong-fire.
- **Defense in depth, measured (H5 falsified productively):** with
  the acquisition gauntlet disabled entirely, ungated proposals still
  produced zero wrong-fire — the runtime certification layer contains
  them — but cost coverage (sem 100→94.7%, adv 83.6→75.4% under
  llama's pool). Gates buy hygiene and coverage; certification is the
  safety floor.
- **Economics:** sonnet's whole mining bill was ~720k tokens (≈$3.5)
  for +7.2pp certified coverage against a residual whose pure-LLM
  cost measured 1,910 J/query locally. Local llama mining costs
  6,973 J/call and bought nothing — mining is worth paying a strong
  model for, occasionally, rather than a weak one constantly.

## 2. What was built (P1–P3)

Gateway + adapters (ollama, OpenAI-compatible, Anthropic direct/
bridge); the ported engine (recognition, semantic state, integrity
certification, worker/planner/paths, escalation) vendored byte-exact
from v0.11 with SHA-256 origins, then made pack-driven: 77 kinds, 58
capabilities, lexicons, B3-complete conversions extracted to
`packs/physics/` with a loader whose injection is verified
byte-identical to the legacy tables. Acquisition: two producers
(breeder, miner) behind ONE gauntlet — static gates (conflict,
redundancy, alias-collision, dimensional verification of proposed
capabilities), shadow probation on held-back real traffic (3
consecutive confirmations, zero wrong-fire AND zero lost coverage),
dependency-coupled promotion (a mined capability activates only with
its mined kind), pack-overlay persistence with per-entry authorship.
Loop-close was demonstrated live before the experiment: a momentum
kind + capability mined from a real llama escalation, dimensionally
certified, promoted, then answering paraphrases deterministically
(14.0, 30.0) with full certificates.

## 3. EXP-ACQ design (pre-registered)

Stream: 45,000 fresh v0.11-recipe items filtered by the frozen
engine — 83.4% answered (zero wrongly), the 7,454 escalations are
the residual universe; disjoint splits val 1,000 / traffic 5,000 /
sealed 500 (+ fresh sem 76 / adv 122 finals). Generator committed
before any item existed; one pre-data capacity amendment recorded.
Arms share the identical frozen engine, gauntlet, and replayed
stream; only enabled producers differ. Shadow pool: every 7th item,
hidden from both producers. Miner: 500-call budget, every 10th
escalation. The ≤2% wrong-fire gate applies to every arm.

## 5. Honest structure of the negative

The residual's item-level anatomy (sampled diagnosis) is dominated by
ENGINE-layer gaps — word-number vocabulary ("a dozen"), hyphenated
units ("2-tonne"), ask-focus mis-naming — which no candidate type can
express. That is why 90%+ of even sonnet's residual stays uncovered,
why llama's shallow restatements achieve nothing, and what makes the
V1.1 worklist concrete: engine-layer expressiveness (the violation
log), then re-run this exact ablation.
